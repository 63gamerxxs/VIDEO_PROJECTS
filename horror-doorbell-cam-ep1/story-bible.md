- Setting: living room, front door with chain, deadbolt, peephole, coat hook, key bowl, wall clock, lamp. Time 11:44 p.m.

- Rules: the feed is 3 minutes ahead of the house. The Double knocks, Eli opens. The feed never shows Eli.
- Unresolved mysteries: who the Double is, why the feed runs ahead, what "I already know" means.
